## What changes
- A `printer:` station (a Windows printer by name) gets 20 seconds to print instead of 8.
- The print helper is built once on the PC and reused, so only the first ticket after installing is slow. If it cannot
  be kept (antivirus, rights), printing works as before.
- A ticket that timed out is tried again by the queue — it was dropped.
- Test print says "Windows took too long to accept the job …" (what to do), never the PowerShell command line.
