## Added before tagging — A406, A407 (migration 125)
- **A406 (cloud):** an owner's password change or reset no longer signs out the tills; an expired till token is answered
  as expired.
- **A407 (cloud + till, migration 125):** a till's sign-in is never lost by accident. The owner's own PIN on a till no
  longer signs the till out; no owner action touches a till's session; a refused till signs itself back in with its
  device secret (tills enrolled earlier receive one on their next renewal); cashiers keep signing in on the till's own
  PIN check meanwhile; ZapTill is emailed (critical) when a till could not sign back in. CI check
  `scripts/check-till-sessions.mjs`.
