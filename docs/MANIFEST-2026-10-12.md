## What changes
- **A453:** admin-portal switch "Only managers record expenses and cash in / out" — the till, the web POS and the cloud
  refuse a cashier (even with a manager's PIN); a manager signed in records them.
- **A454:** a cashier's shift close prints their own slip (their count per method, expenses, pay-outs, kitchen voids — no
  sales, expected or variance); the Z-report prints on the till's Windows receipt printer when no thermal Till station is
  set up.
- **A455:** Reports › Monthly pack — per branch and month: daily sales by payment method with expected / counted /
  variance, the expense book, cash and M-Pesa books (with opening balance, other income, personal, bank deposits, charges
  recorded by a manager); Excel download; emailed on the 1st when switched on in Settings › Report Scheduler.
- **A456:** two receipt copies (Settings › Printers › Copies) on a thermal printer too.
- **A457:** the till's Overview redesigned (figures unchanged).
- **A458:** Close Branch leads with the day's totals per payment method; shifts confirmed one by one; tills behind "Show
  tills"; the manager ticks the totals before closing.
