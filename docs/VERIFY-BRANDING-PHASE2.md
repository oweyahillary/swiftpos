# VERIFY — Branding Phase 2, slice 5: every theme on a real till (A323)

**Who:** the owner, on till **mamangina** (desktop ≥ 0.6.6) and a browser on the web POS. **Business:** B Foods, themes **ON**
(admin portal). **Time:** about 20 minutes. **Why:** the themes were proven by numbers and in a browser; this is the check the
numbers cannot make — how each colour looks on the real screen, in the real room. It also serves as the lighting check for
**A329** (SwiftPOS's own colour becoming teal).

## How to switch theme
Dashboard → Business → **Branding** → **App theme** → pick → **Save branding**. The till follows within ~20 s (no restart).
Keep the brand colour **empty** for the main walk (so the lock screen shows the theme too); step C sets one.

## A. For EACH of the seven themes — Ocean, Violet, Lagoon, Orchid, Sky, Teal, Blossom
On the till, with one item in the cart:

| # | Check | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Charge** | the theme colour; its label reads easily at arm's length |
| A2 | Selected **category** chip and **Dine in** tab | the theme colour, clearly "selected" |
| A3 | **Prices** on the product cards | still **green** |
| A4 | A **Paid** badge / "Shift open" / online dot (anywhere visible) | still **green** — and at a glance you would not mistake **Charge** for a "paid" state |
| A5 | **Lock till** → curtain | PIN dots + Enter in the theme; Enter's text readable |
| A6 | Web POS (browser) → Charge / Add to Order | the same theme as the till |

Record one line per theme: `Ocean: A1–A6 PASS` or the check that failed and what you saw.

## B. The two flagged themes, under your usual shop lighting
**Teal** and **Blossom** sit closest to a status colour (Teal to "paid" green, Blossom to "void" red). With the till where it
normally stands, at normal light:

| # | Check | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Teal: **Charge** next to a green **Paid** badge / "Shift open" | clearly two different colours at a glance |
| B2 | Blossom: **Charge** next to a red **Void** / End Shift | clearly two different colours at a glance |

B1 is also the go / no-go for **A329**: if Teal and "paid" green are too close in your room, say so — the SwiftPOS teal would
then use a deeper shade.

## C. A brand colour with a theme
Set a brand colour (e.g. the orange palette swatch) and keep the theme on Violet → Save. On the till:
C1 thin brand strip across the top · C2 Manager sidebar tinted with it, text readable · C3 Lock-till curtain in the brand colour ·
C4 buttons still Violet. Then clear the brand colour again.

## D. Off again
Admin → **Turn themes off** → the till and web POS return to the standard look (buttons green today; teal once A329 ships).
