## The switches (admin portal → client → Features → POS switches; OFF unless set)
| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen voids need a manager** (`kitchen_void_approval`) | Removing or reducing an item already sent to the kitchen needs a manager — signed in as themselves, or their PIN (no grace period). A shift cannot end while a sent order on it is unpaid (till and web). |
| **Pay before kitchen** (`pay_before_kitchen`) | Takeaway, delivery and counter orders go to the kitchen only when paid — Send to kitchen is not offered; the kitchen ticket prints at payment. Dine-in tables still send first and pay at the end. |

## For every client (no switch)
- **A sent order cannot disappear.** The till records what went to the kitchen (`kitchen_lines`). Clear, a crash or a restart leaves
  it listed — "Sent to the kitchen, not paid" with an **Open** button — until it is charged or voided; End Shift lists them.
- **Taking a sent item back is a kitchen void:** −, ✕, Clear, or deleting a held tab opens it — a reason (wrong item / wrong
  quantity / customer changed their mind / out of stock / kitchen mistake), "had the kitchen already made it?", a note. Items not
  yet sent are removed freely, as before.
- **The kitchen gets a VOID ticket** (kitchen and dispatch stations) so it stops cooking.
- **The Z-report** (till screen and paper, web EOD) lists every kitchen void — item, reason, made or not, who approved, cashier —
  the total, and the part already made (food wasted).
- **A373 — the kitchen no longer cooks a dish twice.** A line remembers how many are on a ticket; Send prints only the extra (a line
  of 2 tapped to 3 sends 1, not 3). Payment first sends anything not yet sent (a line added after the last Send used to never reach
  the kitchen). A note goes only on a line the kitchen has not seen.
- **Web POS:** the same on its open orders — a sent line is reduced or removed only through the kitchen void (the cloud recomputes the
  order; nothing left → the order is voided); a sent tab cannot be dropped.

## Not in this build
- Food already made and then voided is **reported**, not deducted from stock as waste.
- A branch node does not copy kitchen voids between tills (each till pushes its own to the cloud).
- A dine-in tab still open at shift change blocks End Shift with the switch on — it must be paid or voided (no "hand over to the
  next shift" yet; say if you want it).
