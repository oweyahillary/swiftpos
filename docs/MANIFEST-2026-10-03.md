# MANIFEST 2026-10-03 — desktop 0.6.34: the business day ends at the owner's cut-off (A382)

**Base:** origin/dev `799bb77`. **Delivered as:** `swiftpos-2026-10-03-v0.6.34.patch` — includes the version bump
(apps/desktop and `shared/release.ts` → 0.6.34). **No migration** (a business setting). Cloud, web and till; local schema 64
(the cloud then requires it).

Found in the lead review (2026-10-03): a hotel bar trading past midnight was locked at 00:00 — "Trading day … was never
closed. A manager must count the cash and close it" — mid-service; with 24-hour operation, at 02:00.

## What changes
- **Settings › Business › Business day ends at** — 00:00 (midnight, the default: nothing changes) to 06:00 in half hours.
  A branch may override it (Branches › the branch › overrides). A bar open until 3 am sets 04:00.
- **The till:** a sale before the cut-off belongs to the night before. The till keeps selling through the night; after the
  cut-off the day must be closed, as before (a manager). With 24-hour operation the 2-hour grace runs from the cut-off.
  History's "Today" shows the whole night.
- **The cloud:** every dashboard report and the daily email count the business day (cut-off to cut-off).
- **The web POS:** History's "today" follows it (the web POS has no day lock).
- **Not changed:** the dashboard's date pickers default to the calendar date — a report opened between midnight and the
  cut-off shows the new (empty) day; pick yesterday.

## Files
NEW `shared/businessDay.ts` (+ copies `apps/server/src/lib`, `apps/desktop/src/main`, `apps/dashboard/src/lib`;
`scripts/check-shared-sync.mjs`), `apps/server/src/lib/dayCutoff.ts`, `apps/dashboard/src/lib/webDayCutoff.ts`,
`tests/business-day.test.mjs` (8), `apps/desktop/test/business-day.test.mjs` (14; CI step). CHANGED: cloud
`routes/business.ts`, `routes/branches.ts`, `routes/pos.ts`, `routes/reports.ts`, `jobs/dailySummary.ts`,
`lib/desktopSchema.ts` (64); till `main/dayService.ts`, `deviceConfig.ts`, `localDb.ts` (64), `syncEngine.ts`,
`referenceBundle.ts`, `managerReports.ts`; web `settings/BusinessProfileTab.tsx`, `settings/BranchReceiptOverrides.tsx`,
`pos/POSOrderHistoryTab.tsx`, `pos/cashier/usePOSData.ts`; version 0.6.34; `.github/workflows/ci.yml`;
`docs/AUDIT-REGISTER.md` (A382), this file.

## Rollout (owner)
1. Apply, commit, push to dev; CI green. Merge to main (cloud and web deploy; no migration). Tag v0.6.34 from dev.
2. Approve 0.6.34 for a test client; T1 updates.
3. Settings › Business › Business day ends at **04:00**. On T1 (after its next sync): open a shift in the evening; at
   00:30 the till still sells; History shows the evening's sales; after 04:00 it asks a manager to close the day.
4. Set it back to 00:00 → the till locks at midnight again, as before.

# desktop 0.6.37: what cashiers see in History (A387); a manager approves pay outs and expenses (A388)

**Base:** origin/dev `6386eb6`. **Delivered as:** `swiftpos-2026-10-03-v0.6.37.patch` — includes the version bump
(apps/desktop and `shared/release.ts` → 0.6.37). **Migration 118** (apply BEFORE the cloud deploys). Cloud, web and till;
local schema 66 (the cloud then requires it).

## What changes
- **History (A387):** a manager picks which payment methods a cashier's History shows — the till (Manager → Settings →
  What cashiers see in History), the web (Settings › Business), and a branch may have its own (Branches › overrides).
  Every box ticked (the default) = every method, as before. A split sale shows a cashier only its ticked part and that

  Expenses list shows it.

- **ZapTill names (A386 follow-up):** every file the web downloads is `zaptill…` (report exports, import templates,
  the admin portal's client export); a till that reports no name is "ZapTill till" (one already stored as "SwiftPOS
  till" is still recognised, and Settings › Devices shows it as ZapTill). Kept: `SwiftPOS-PrintServer.exe` (the print
  server's real file name — renaming it is its own change).
- **Till › Menu › Import:** no sample menu — the "Download a sample file" button and the sample rows are gone; the
  column explanations stay.

# 2026-10-04: stock take — blind counts, review, the owner's freeze (A394)

**Base:** origin/dev `03890be` **plus** `swiftpos-2026-10-04-otp-alerts-admin.patch` (apply that one first).
**Delivered as:** `swiftpos-2026-10-04-stock-take.patch`. **Migration 120** (the DB migrate workflow applies it on main).
**Local till schema 67** — the till part (an item being counted cannot be sold) ships with the next desktop build; no
version bump in this patch. Tills on 0.6.37 keep trading and syncing; they simply do not stop selling a frozen item (the
count still allows for their sales).

## What changes
- **Stock › Stock Counts** (owner) and **Inventory › Stock count** (manager, with the new `inventory.count` right —
  given to manager roles by migration 120). Start a count at a branch: stocked products, ingredients or both;
  everything the branch stocks or chosen categories.
- **Blind.** Whoever counts types what is on the shelf and never sees the system's figure — on the count screen, on the
  printed sheet, and (while the branch counts) on the manager's stock screens.
- **Hand in → review → post.** The owner sees every difference and its value (short / over / net), can send items back
  to be counted again, and posts: stock changes by the differences only; each change is a movement named after the count
  (ST-0001 …). Print the differences for the file.
- **Selling while counting.** Allowed unless the owner freezes; each item is compared with what the system held when it
  was counted, and a till's sale made before the count that syncs afterwards is allowed for.
- **Freeze (owner's switch on Stock Counts, recommended).** An item in a count cannot be sold until it has been counted
  — refused on the web POS now, and on the tills from the next desktop build. Counted items are released at once.
- The Inventory page's printed count sheet no longer shows the system quantity (blind).

## After deploy (owner)
1. Stock › Stock Counts: choose whether to freeze (the switch at the top).
2. Start a small count (one category) at a branch; count a few items as a manager; hand in; review as the owner; post.
3. Check the product's stock moved by the difference only, and its movement says "Stock take ST-0001".

# 2026-10-04: what is owed to suppliers — bills, payments, returns (A395)

**Base:** origin/dev `03890be` **plus** the OTP patch and the stock take patch (apply those first, in that order).
**Delivered as:** `swiftpos-2026-10-04-supplier-payables.patch`. **Migration 121** (the DB migrate workflow applies it on
main). **No till change, no version bump** — the cloud and the dashboard deploy with the merge.

## What changes
- **Stock › Suppliers** (owner): what you owe all suppliers, how much is overdue, what falls due in the next 7 days, and
  each supplier's balance. **Account** opens a supplier.
- **Bills** — the supplier's invoice number, amount, date and "pay within" terms (due date worked out). Deliveries
  received against the supplier's purchase orders and not yet billed are listed with their value — "Bill it".
- **Payments** — M-Pesa, bank, cash, cheque or other, with a reference; against a bill (never more than it still owes)
  or on account. Not an expense, not from a drawer.
- **Return goods** — pick products / ingredients and quantities; the stock leaves the branch now and the supplier owes
  you the credit (at cost).
- **Statement** with a running balance (print it for the supplier), and ageing by days late.
- Only the owner sees money owed and records bills / payments (new right "Supplier bills & payments", given to nobody
  by default). A manager who receives deliveries may send goods back.

## After deploy (owner)
1. Stock › Suppliers: open a supplier › Add bill (or "Bill it" on a delivery).
2. Record a payment against it — the bill shows part paid / paid, the balance drops.
3. Return goods for one item — its stock drops and the statement shows the credit.
