job runs it on merge to main). Till: A401 only — build and release 0.6.39 for it.

## What changes
- **A400 — combos:** selling a combo (Menu › Combos) takes each of its items out of stock and uses their recipes'
  ingredients; the food-cost report counts them. Meals loaded as plain products need to be made combos (or given a
  recipe) to count.
- **A401 — force-close:** a drawer force-closed (or closed) on the web closes on the till within ~20 s; the till says
  who closed it and why, and selling needs a new shift.
- **A402 — passwords:** "Forgot password?" on the dashboard sign-in page (emailed 6-digit code, then a new password);
  Settings › My sign-in › Password (change it, signed in). Owners only — managers keep email + PIN.
- **A403 — sign-in codes:** someone on an authenticator app can press "Email me a code instead".
